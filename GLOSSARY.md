# MyLeagueTracker

A companion for EA FC career-mode playthroughs: it records the careers a manager runs, the squads they build, and the competitions they play.

## Language

### Career & Squads

**Career**:
A single playthrough of one manager at one club in one EA FC title.
_Avoid_: Save, save game, campaign

**Career Squad**:
The pair of rosters — principal squad and youth academy — that belongs to exactly one career.
_Avoid_: Team, roster (when the whole pair is meant)

**Principal Squad**:
The senior players available to a career's manager for matches.
_Avoid_: First team, main team, elenco principal

**Youth Player**:
A player who belongs to a career's youth academy and is not yet available to the principal squad.
_Avoid_: Prospect, academy player, youngster

**Player**:
A footballer in the shared catalog of real-world players, independent of any career.
_Avoid_: Catalog player (redundant)

**Squad Player**:
A player's membership in one career's principal squad, carrying the career-specific state of that player.
_Avoid_: Player (when the membership is meant), contract

**Squad Role**:
The manager's stated importance for a squad player, from crucial to prospect.
_Avoid_: Status, priority

### Career Structure

**Season**:
One football year within a career, spanning a start year and an end year.
_Avoid_: Campaign, ano, temporada (as an English synonym)

**Club**:
A football team that a career is played at, or that a transfer moves a player to or from.
_Avoid_: Team (when the club entity is meant)

**Game Title**:
The EA FC release a career is played in (EA FC 24, EA FC 25, EA FC 26).
_Avoid_: Version, edition, game

**Trophy**:
A competition outcome recorded against a season — a team placing or an individual award.
_Avoid_: Title, medal, conquista

**Transfer**:
A player movement between clubs recorded within a career and a season.
_Avoid_: Negotiation, deal

### Product Screens

**Dashboard**:
The screen that lists the signed-in manager's careers and offers to start a new one.
_Avoid_: Home, career mode, careers list

**Central Squad**:
The in-career screen for browsing the principal squad, listing players and showing one player's detail.
_Avoid_: Squad hub, squad page, central

**Player Statistics**:
The in-career table of per-player season performance (appearances, goals, assists, cards, rating).
This data is not yet recorded; the screen is deferred until it exists.
_Avoid_: Stats (when the screen is meant)

**Youth Academy**:
The in-career screen for browsing and promoting youth players.
_Avoid_: Academy, youth squad

### Access

**Manager**:
The in-game persona recorded as leading a career's club.
_Avoid_: User, account, coach

**User**:
The authenticated person who signs in and owns one or more careers.
_Avoid_: Manager (when the owner is meant), account, login
